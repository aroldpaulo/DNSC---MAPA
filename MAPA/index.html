    School image placeholder

    Goal

    On Curriculum and Instruction: To Cultivate a Dynamic and Inclusive Learning Environment for a Globalized Future
    On Research, Development, and Extension: To Foster a Culture of Innovation and Collaboration in Research, Development, and Extension
    On Administration and Resource Management: To Enhance Institutional Effectiveness and Sustainability through Integrated Initiatives

    Mission

    DNSC shall produce future-ready workforce, create innovative solutions and technologies, empower communities, and uphold good governance towards sustainable development.

    Vision

    An Institution Leading in Agri-Fisheries and Socio-Cultural Development in the ASEAN Region

    Core Values

    Stewardship, Adaptability and Excellence, Integrity and Innovativeness, and Love of God and Country
